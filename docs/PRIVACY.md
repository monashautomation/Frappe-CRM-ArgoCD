# Privacy Policy

**Monash Automation CRM** (`https://crm.monashautomation.com`)
Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Monash Automation CRM ("the Service") collects, how it is used, and your choices. The Service is an internal customer relationship management system run by Monash Automation ("we", "us").

## 1. Who this applies to

- **Staff and members** who log in to the Service through Monash Automation's single sign-on.
- **Contacts** (for example partners, sponsors and prospects) whose details are stored in the CRM or who exchange emails with us through it.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account details** from our identity provider when you log in: name, email address, username and the groups you belong to (used to decide your access level).
- **CRM data** that staff enter or that arrives by email: contact names, organisations, email addresses, phone numbers, notes, and the emails and attachments exchanged with contacts.
- **Technical data** such as logs and timestamps needed to run and secure the Service.

## 3. Google user data

The Service connects to one shared Google mailbox (`partnerships.automation@monash.edu`) using Google OAuth so that it can read incoming emails and send emails from that address.

- We access only that mailbox, and only the data needed to receive and send CRM emails.
- Emails read from the mailbox are stored in the CRM and shown to authorised staff against the related contact, lead or deal.
- We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties except as needed to run the Service or as required by law.
- We do not allow humans to read the data unless needed for security, to comply with law, or with the mailbox owner's consent, other than authorised staff using the CRM as intended.
- The Service's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 4. How we use information

- To operate the CRM and let staff manage relationships and correspondence.
- To control who can log in and what they can do.
- To keep the Service secure and fix problems.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

- **Service providers** that host and run the Service (for example our hosting, identity provider and email providers), under their own terms.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.

## 6. Storage and security

Data is stored in our own hosted infrastructure. Access is limited by role, protected by single sign-on, and credentials are kept in a secrets manager. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## 7. Retention

We keep CRM records and emails while they are needed for our work, then delete or anonymise them. Ask us if you want something removed sooner.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. To revoke the Service's access to the Google mailbox, remove it at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions> or ask us to disconnect it.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
